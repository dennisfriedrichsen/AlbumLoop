# Stillroom minimal ribbon

Created with built-in imagegen. Reference: https://jellyfin.org/docs/general/contributing/branding/

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a radically simplified, sophisticated app icon concept for Stillroom, an Apple TV personal photo slideshow app. User disliked cartoon mountains and suns and wants a cleaner aesthetic inspired by Jellyfin's economy of geometry and purple-to-blue gradient. Design an ORIGINAL symbol: one broad continuous ribbon forms a softly rounded rectangular empty picture frame, with a single elegant half-twist at the center of its bottom edge. The interior is completely empty negative space. Flat vector-like graphic, precise smooth geometry, restrained violet at upper left flowing through periwinkle into cyan at lower right. Twist indicated only by a clean overlap and subtle two-tone gradient, no physical material rendering. Calm, minimal, mature identity, bold silhouette, generous negative space. Center symbol on uniform very dark midnight navy full-bleed background, landscape 5:3 canvas. Symbol occupies about 55 percent of canvas width and 65 percent height. No landscapes, mountains, suns, photos, illustration, carousel, text, letters, shadows, bevels, glass, shine, bloom, texture, 3D extrusion or mockups. Do not use Jellyfin's triangular outline or nested triangles, no play triangle. Single icon only.

## Cleanup prompt

Clean up this Stillroom icon. Preserve exactly the simple purple-to-cyan rounded rectangular ribbon frame and its bottom-center twist, empty interior, composition and aspect ratio. Replace every background pixel, both outside and inside the frame, with solid opaque midnight navy #101426. Remove ALL scattered neon speckles, fringe artifacts and noise around the symbol. Smooth clean antialiased edges. Absolutely no transparency. Flat graphic, no extra objects, text, highlights, scenery or shadows.
